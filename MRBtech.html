How India Shaped the Modern World

Timeless inventions and wisdom from ancient India that continue to power our global civilization

What is the major mathematical invention from India that the world uses?

Zero (Śūnya) was conceptualized and used in mathematical operations in ancient India (Aryabhata, Brahmagupta ~5th–7th century CE). It is the foundation of the decimal positional system and modern computing.

What is the origin of Yoga, practiced worldwide for health and wellness?

Yoga originated in ancient India over 5,000 years ago and was systematized in the Yoga Sutras of Patanjali. Today it is practiced by hundreds of millions for physical, mental, and spiritual well-being.

Where was the game of Chess invented?

Chess was invented in India as Chaturanga during the Gupta Empire (~6th century CE). It later spread to Persia and Europe, evolving into the modern strategic game.

Who invented Shampoo?

The word "shampoo" derives from the Hindi/Sanskrit "chāmpo" meaning to massage. Ancient Indians developed herbal hair cleansing formulations centuries before it reached Europe.

Where were buttons first used?

The earliest known functional buttons date back to the Indus Valley Civilization (~2800–2000 BCE), used both as fasteners and ornaments — a simple yet revolutionary invention.
